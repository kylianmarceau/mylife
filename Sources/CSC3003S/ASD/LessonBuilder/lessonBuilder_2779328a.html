Welcome to Week 1

  • Hi. Welcome to your first week of CSC3003S and to Advanced Software Development. This week, in addition to engagement with the lectures, one of your key activities will be to pick your capstone projects and groups. You should expect to meet with your client on Friday or early next week to learn more about your assigned project. 

    In addition, please make sure to review the Week 0 background material, summarizing some key concepts from INF2009F which you will be expected to make use of for the Capstone project. To help you self-assess and prepare, we have included in this week some activities, as well as a quiz which will be for marks.
