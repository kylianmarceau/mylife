## Suggested Study Order

1. Start with Aslam's [Introduction And Computer System Structure](ASLAM/01_Introduction_And_Computer_System_Structure.md) and [Operating System Services And Structures](ASLAM/02_Operating_System_Services_And_Structures.md) to get the OS vocabulary, system-call model, and kernel structure.
2. Study Michel's [Processes](MICHEL/01_Processes.md), because scheduling, synchronization, and deadlock all assume process/thread state and PCB terminology.
3. Study Michel's [Process Scheduling](MICHEL/02_Process_Scheduling.md) and practise the Gantt-chart calculations.
4. Study Michel's [Process Synchronization](MICHEL/03_Process_Synchronization.md) before [Deadlock](MICHEL/04_Deadlock.md), because deadlock is a liveness failure caused by resource/synchronization interactions.
5. Study Aslam's [Main Memory](ASLAM/03_Main_Memory.md), then continue with [Demand Paging And Page Replacement](ASLAM/04_Demand_Paging_And_Page_Replacement.md) and [Frame Allocation, Thrashing, And Working Sets](ASLAM/05_Frame_Allocation_Thrashing_And_Working_Sets.md).
6. Finish Aslam's section with [Security](ASLAM/06_Security.md) and [Protection](ASLAM/07_Protection.md).
