### Public Narratives And Grounded Evaluation

<!-- qard-id: ais-1ac1d644e96614b6dc57 -->
> [!qard]- What is generative AI?
> AI producing content such as text, code, and images from supplied context.

<!-- qard-id: ais-d968e0a3ced488fe8266 -->
> [!qard]- What is business value?
> Improvement to an operational outcome that matters to the client.

<!-- qard-id: ais-3fc773d8b8a7120c4b4c -->
> [!qard]- What concerns do the opening AI narratives illustrate?
> Spending/hype without task value, unreliable autonomous actions, dramatic risk narratives, and fabricated factual citations.

<!-- qard-id: ais-3940a66f9067fe8e4cb3 -->
> [!qard]- Should lecture screenshots be treated as independently verified incident reports or current forecasts?
> No. They motivate careful deployment without establishing universal behavior or verified current claims.

<!-- qard-id: ais-a32e87bf64e541bd2240 -->
> [!qard]- What practical deployment process does the lecture advocate?
> Identify a suitable problem, integrate AI into a useful workflow, review outputs, and measure impact.

### Consolidating Information For A New Client

<!-- qard-id: ais-322039d1612c985c9a2e -->
> [!qard]- What is consolidation?
> Combining dispersed information into a coherent, inspectable account.

<!-- qard-id: ais-455adab1214ba2c5a0df -->
> [!qard]- What are domain knowledge and a pain point?
> Understanding the client’s subject and operations; a specific operational difficulty or inefficiency.

<!-- qard-id: ais-168ea041ae09887e395d -->
> [!qard]- Why can early client discovery be difficult?
> Context may be sparse, the domain unfamiliar, and the client unsure what solution is needed.

<!-- qard-id: ais-90e4ba25bd6cf703d37f -->
> [!qard]- What can AI contribute to discovery, and what remains expert work?
> Consolidate workshops, deep dives, and documents; experts still identify evidence-supported opportunities and business value.

<!-- qard-id: ais-15355a092f329a54d676 -->
> [!qard]- What is the progression of the consulting opportunity funnel?
> Gather evidence, perform AI-assisted analysis with expert work, map metrics/pain points, generate ideas, identify opportunities, and select priorities.

<!-- qard-id: ais-f9d214393157e95fba76 -->
> [!qard]- Why are the case study’s funnel quantities not a prescribed productivity multiplier?
> They describe one workflow and substantial expert input rather than a controlled universal staffing or productivity relationship.

### Prompting For Opportunity Discovery

<!-- qard-id: ais-2af100347a11b654afe1 -->
> [!qard]- What are impact and implementation effort/feasibility?
> Impact is expected improvement; effort and feasibility describe delivery work and practical constraints.

<!-- qard-id: ais-47ee18055dc74ba788e1 -->
> [!qard]- What should an opportunity-discovery prompt ask the model to inspect?
> Supplied documents for explicit/implicit inefficiencies, technological gaps, and operational challenges.

<!-- qard-id: ais-105ae761e15b78ed69c0 -->
> [!qard]- Which opportunity categories and time horizons should be considered?
> Automation, customer experience, products/services, and cost reduction; both immediate tactical and longer-term strategic opportunities.

<!-- qard-id: ais-03fff6c79a03b5bfe0cf -->
> [!qard]- What fields should a structured opportunity contain?
> Pain point, proposed improvement, appropriate solution type, business impact, complexity/feasibility, and justified impact/effort scores.

<!-- qard-id: ais-3706693b840c9f2e7589 -->
> [!qard]- How should an opportunity’s impact connect to evidence?
> Link it to relevant workshop/deep-dive metrics and explain how the proposed mechanism improves the actual problem.

<!-- qard-id: ais-ae67a30b2f53e78987aa -->
> [!qard]- Why must prioritization scores define their scale and assumptions?
> Scores are otherwise uninterpretable; the source gives no universal ranking formula.

<!-- qard-id: ais-cb3739794118f62367dc -->
> [!qard]- Does an impressive generated opportunity list settle which projects should be built?
> No. It starts expert assessment of evidence, value, feasibility, and technique suitability.

### Structured Opportunities And Expert Selection

<!-- qard-id: ais-c7ab3785fc8594db49d4 -->
> [!qard]- What problem-mechanism-value pattern should an opportunity proposal follow?
> Identify a specific operational problem, explain a suitable proposed solution, and connect that mechanism to measurable client value.

<!-- qard-id: ais-eefba77abd2a669aa130 -->
> [!qard]- What general workflow lesson comes from the report-builder opportunity?
> Validated templates and governed authoring can standardize high-value deliverables instead of relying on inconsistent personal-tool outputs.

<!-- qard-id: ais-16f3c8cb2662d220cf7b -->
> [!qard]- What general workflow lesson comes from the research-recommendation opportunity?
> Retrieve relevant historical research/benchmarks by engagement context, let the consultant select, then generate or insert content.

<!-- qard-id: ais-a51515472a73996548e8 -->
> [!qard]- What does expert opportunity review check?
> Evidence support, whether AI is appropriate, and whether the proposed solution can realistically be delivered.

### Demos And AI-Generated Presentation Content

<!-- qard-id: ais-1f33ff864a347070b1d2 -->
> [!qard]- What is a demo or prototype?
> A limited implementation exploring or demonstrating an idea.

<!-- qard-id: ais-7373c80b02e4c9eaa866 -->
> [!qard]- What is vibe coding?
> Informal prompt-driven software creation with substantial reliance on generated code.

<!-- qard-id: ais-7161ce3ff97aacfb44bf -->
> [!qard]- Why is rapid AI-assisted prototyping useful but insufficient for production?
> It makes an idea concrete without establishing complete requirements or reliable production behavior.

<!-- qard-id: ais-96023a7070f1cd5e4006 -->
> [!qard]- Which inputs should presentation-drafting instructions specify?
> Source content, logical grouping, audience, professional style, and suitable wording/images/tone.

<!-- qard-id: ais-b997bb8bb497ba0c1406 -->
> [!qard]- Why review presentation drafts despite style instructions?
> Models can retain dramatic language or unsupported claims; review factual support, tone, audience fit, and structure.

<!-- qard-id: ais-5e864f3ce75d60d7d3bf -->
> [!qard]- Does improving a draft’s style correct unsupported content?
> No. Factual support requires separate checking.

### Legal-Document Review As A Search-Assistance Task

<!-- qard-id: ais-7bd88b41aa15f288f208 -->
> [!qard]- What is OCR?
> Convert scanned or image-based text into machine-readable text.

<!-- qard-id: ais-426ff48bf3b048230980 -->
> [!qard]- What is fuzzy matching?
> Match text approximately instead of requiring exact equality.

<!-- qard-id: ais-48ccdf78db4a4d1f72ff -->
> [!qard]- What is human review in this workflow?
> A responsible expert verifies findings and their source evidence before accepting the assessment.

<!-- qard-id: ais-f17c3c7e28073decff24 -->
> [!qard]- What is the AI document-review system’s role?
> Help experts locate relevant clauses and pages faster, rather than replace their interpretation of the actual document.

<!-- qard-id: ais-e5b1cafd666d0eb0bc91 -->
> [!qard]- Why must cited clauses be checked against the actual instrument?
> A generated answer and location can be wrong; example-document answers are not general legal rules.

### Initial And Later Implementations

<!-- qard-id: ais-0f071833d515493910f9 -->
> [!qard]- What components formed the initial document-review workflow?
> Local Python OCR, fuzzy matching of common phrases, a small local LLM for hard cases, and human confirmation.

<!-- qard-id: ais-fcf7a9e441dac1d1da83 -->
> [!qard]- What weaknesses did the first document-review implementation have?
> It missed some clauses and could retrieve unrelated clauses sharing similar surface wording.

<!-- qard-id: ais-c4f7149230792cde6210 -->
> [!qard]- What improvement was reported for the later LLM-based analysis?
> Fewer missed clauses, more contextually accurate findings, and faster human checks, with review retained.

<!-- qard-id: ais-2e719850b3cc3948db98 -->
> [!qard]- Why can contextual interpretation improve on phrase matching?
> Similar wording can have different meanings; context helps narrow results to the relevant clause.

<!-- qard-id: ais-96893964fdb5ce311515 -->
> [!qard]- What evidence limitations qualify the later implementation comparison?
> The source does not give the later model, exact prompt, dataset, formal accuracy measurements, or controlled study methodology.

<!-- qard-id: ais-add32a9aa86e6af48508 -->
> [!qard]- Does this case prove every LLM system beats fuzzy matching?
> No. It supports the reported case-specific workflow comparison only.

### Measuring Client Impact

<!-- qard-id: ais-b2808e8c2a4cb3a04b38 -->
> [!qard]- What are a success metric and a baseline?
> A defined measure of the outcome to improve, and performance before the change or under a reference process.

<!-- qard-id: ais-9de16410c23cf6e16ead -->
> [!qard]- What metric was agreed for the document-review case?
> Time taken to vet a document, including the assisted human workflow.

<!-- qard-id: ais-1ced5a19e156e74feb41 -->
> [!qard]- What is the speedup formula for before/after times $T_0,T_1$?
> $\operatorname{speedup}=T_0/T_1$.

<!-- qard-id: ais-256118798634d7522be5 -->
> [!qard]- What is the time-reduction percentage formula?
> $\operatorname{time\ reduction}=(T_0-T_1)/T_0\times100\%$.

<!-- qard-id: ais-61385bb385a7ee7617b6 -->
> [!qard]- Why measure quality alongside review speed?
> Faster checking is not useful if evidence accuracy degrades or important clauses are missed.

<!-- qard-id: ais-3623fc87b286c7b14ad2 -->
> [!qard]- What process produces a meaningful client-impact comparison?
> Agree the metric before building, record representative baselines, measure under comparable conditions, include review/correction time, and assess quality as well as speed.

<!-- qard-id: ais-2feb83939c7417d4ca07 -->
> [!qard]- Why include time spent checking and correcting AI output?
> It is part of the actual assisted workflow and can offset apparent generation speed gains.

<!-- qard-id: ais-e856b496c9a3dbecea5a -->
> [!qard]- Why do case-study time reductions alone not establish a controlled universal effect?
> The source lacks sample size and review-time distributions; results depend on the tested documents and process.

### Choosing An Appropriate Solution

<!-- qard-id: ais-2333776bf9b5091af934 -->
> [!qard]- Why should conventional rules, search, or simple automation remain options?
> The technique should fit the problem; AI usage itself is not the objective.

<!-- qard-id: ais-8b0f25063abd81f98f7b -->
> [!qard]- Why retain expert review in consequential document interpretation?
> The system finds evidence, while the responsible reviewer verifies and accepts the interpretation.

<!-- qard-id: ais-abaac54fbbbd1514eeed -->
> [!qard]- What makes a generative-AI solution appropriate?
> Fit to task, workflow, and evaluation, supported by measurable outcomes and output review.
