## Core Content

### Professional Responsibility

<!-- qard-id: ap-5f985d227e482ee5ec51 -->
> [!qard]- What safeguards does the lecture associate with a regulated profession?
> Formal competence requirements, supervised practice, continuing education, oversight and enforceable disciplinary accountability.

<!-- qard-id: ap-08439d3e752fc3f358d6 -->
> [!qard]- What common priorities appear in the ACM and IEEE ethics codes discussed here?
> Public welfare, avoiding harm, honesty, competence, fairness, privacy, respect for others' work and responsible technical practice.

<!-- qard-id: ap-d75c2ce2f47d7e471c49 -->
> [!qard]- How should a professional handle work beyond their competence?
> Disclose limitations, obtain appropriate expertise/training and avoid presenting unqualified work as dependable.

<!-- qard-id: ap-c6e9877e6eb9e1dc32dd -->
> [!qard]- Why does ethical judgement require more than finding a loophole in a written code?
> Codes express principles and intent; an action can exploit literal wording while still harming people or violating professional duties.

<!-- qard-id: ap-278cf27a0c040e37e9a9 -->
> [!qard]- Why does legal permission alone not settle whether an action is ethical?
> Ethical assessment also considers harm, fairness, rights and professional responsibilities; law and moral justification answer different questions.

<!-- qard-id: ap-10d718626138a4b17330 -->
> [!qard]- How can a software case be assessed against an ethics code?
> Identify affected stakeholders and harms, map concrete decisions to duties such as competence/honesty/safety, and justify alternatives using the principles.

<!-- qard-id: ap-030ba4465115cc9f6fb5 -->
> [!qard]- What should a professional do about a conflict of interest?
> Identify and disclose it so decisions can be evaluated transparently rather than silently biased by competing interests.

### Attribution And Reuse

<!-- qard-id: ap-9b4a9a946cbee6354b47 -->
> [!qard]- How do copyright and a software licence relate?
> Copyright establishes rights over a work; a licence states permissions and conditions for using, modifying or distributing it.

<!-- qard-id: ap-0477f13b92fbc81ac6da -->
> [!qard]- What is the basic idea of copyleft?
> Use licensing conditions to preserve recipients' freedoms when covered software is redistributed or derived works are distributed, within the licence's scope.

<!-- qard-id: ap-6f90b05209cc3e6cdfd9 -->
> [!qard]- How do permissive and copyleft licences differ in broad purpose?
> Permissive licences allow broad reuse with relatively few conditions; copyleft licences attach continuing sharing/freedom obligations to covered redistribution.

<!-- qard-id: ap-3ffbf670b7d67339a5ad -->
> [!qard]- Why is unattributed code reuse a professional problem as well as dishonesty?
> It hides provenance, assumptions and obligations, making limitations harder to investigate and potentially violating others' rights.

<!-- qard-id: ap-3a560333b80351015afd -->
> [!qard]- Why does declaring AI or other assistance matter for authorship?
> It makes contributions and claimed understanding transparent and permits evaluation under the task's applicable rules.

### ACM Code Of Ethics (2018)

<!-- qard-id: ap-05f7cd194e693c0a9bb6 -->
> [!qard]- What additional responsibilities does the ACM code assign to professionals and leaders beyond avoiding harm?
> Maintain competence and quality, seek/provide review, evaluate system impacts, respect privacy/confidentiality and authorised access, and centre public welfare in organisational policy and changes to important infrastructure.

### IEEE Code Of Ethics (2020)

<!-- qard-id: ap-e2a155ff0ef9e9a0b0d6 -->
> [!qard]- How do honesty, criticism, fairness and reporting duties apply in the IEEE principles discussed here?
> Make realistic claims/estimates, acknowledge and correct mistakes, credit contributions, reject bribery/harassment/discrimination, and support rather than retaliate against good-faith ethical reporting.

### Licensing Of Software Professionals?

<!-- qard-id: ap-9fcfcf98f03a35da9e9b -->
> [!qard]- What arguments does the lecture weigh for and against licensing software professionals?
> It reports concerns about premature licensing and uncertain quality benefit, against the case for minimum competence and enforceable accountability. Licensing cannot itself prevent every failure or ethical violation.

### Case: Boeing MCAS

<!-- qard-id: ap-acb2eb1326253ff1665c -->
> [!qard]- Which ethical questions should be asked about the MCAS case rather than judging only whether code compiled?
> Was public safety protected, expertise adequate, risk communicated honestly, design quality sufficient, and modification of an existing system handled responsibly? Connect evidence to those duties.

### Case: Generative AI And Meta's LLM

<!-- qard-id: ap-1a10f08c76fb05ec8434 -->
> [!qard]- What ethical issue does the lecture use the Meta training-data case to illustrate?
> Claimed legal permission does not settle respect for creators' work, provenance and broader harm. The lecture argues for ethical accountability; separate that judgement from any independently established legal finding.

### Stallman, Copyleft And Sci-Hub

<!-- qard-id: ap-02f0b7739dea35da3001 -->
> [!qard]- What competing principles do the Stallman and Sci-Hub discussions raise?
> User freedom and access to knowledge versus creators' rights and existing restrictions. Stallman's advocacy of boycott differs from endorsing piracy; analyse law reform/civil-disobedience arguments separately from current legal permission.

### Common Software Licences

<!-- qard-id: ap-6685d8c720745b68cf85 -->
> [!qard]- How are MIT/Apache, GPL and LGPL characterised in the note?
> MIT/Apache are permissive (Apache includes a patent grant); GPL uses stronger copyleft, and LGPL provides more limited library-focused copyleft. These are study-level distinctions; actual reuse obligations depend on the precise licence and use.

### Ethical Duties

<!-- qard-id: ap-ca7b17263fb3324abf28 -->
> [!qard]- How does privacy differ from confidentiality?
> Privacy concerns appropriate collection/use and respect for people; confidentiality concerns protecting entrusted information from improper disclosure. A confidentially stored collection can still be inappropriate to collect/use.

<!-- qard-id: ap-d72335755dae94a4ab05 -->
> [!qard]- What does honest communication require of an estimate or safety claim?
> State realistic evidence, assumptions, uncertainty and limitations rather than presenting guesses or incomplete checks as guaranteed results.

<!-- qard-id: ap-543939f1c1703a7fb810 -->
> [!qard]- Why is seeking competent review an ethical responsibility?
> Important mistakes can affect others; independent scrutiny and correction help protect public welfare beyond the author's individual confidence.

<!-- qard-id: ap-ea418594ac9c3839e3be -->
> [!qard]- Why should a professional disclose a real or apparent conflict of interest?
> Others need to evaluate competing incentives and the integrity of decisions, even before a concrete harmful outcome occurs.

<!-- qard-id: ap-590cdf484c19bb3d96a7 -->
> [!qard]- What distinguishes following a code's intent from exploiting its literal omissions?
> Apply its guiding responsibilities to the actual stakeholders and consequences; absence of a named forbidden action does not automatically justify it.

<!-- qard-id: ap-dd27188c99908b4f6caf -->
> [!qard]- Why are leaders' responsibilities broader than writing personally correct code?
> Their policies, incentives, resources and system-change decisions shape others' conduct and the risks imposed on the public.

<!-- qard-id: ap-709c313a4703a56a0a2e -->
> [!qard]- Why protect colleagues who report ethical concerns in good faith?
> Retaliation suppresses evidence of harm and undermines professional accountability, even when investigating the concern is inconvenient.

### Competence, Reuse And Accountability

<!-- qard-id: ap-a1044ebf2357dfb99d31 -->
> [!qard]- How does continuing education contribute to professional competence?
> Tools, threats and practices change, so initial qualification alone does not ensure current ability to undertake a particular task responsibly.

<!-- qard-id: ap-226a4d0a8b1e381a910f -->
> [!qard]- Why does acknowledging an error require more than removing evidence of it?
> Correct the problem, communicate relevant consequences and preserve appropriate accountability, rather than concealing a risk to others.

<!-- qard-id: ap-060c8d4094f99903e8ba -->
> [!qard]- How does plagiarism undermine traceability of reused code?
> It hides the original context, assumptions, known defects and licensing/provenance, making safe investigation and maintenance harder.

<!-- qard-id: ap-df6f247bc4c6c05bb605 -->
> [!qard]- Why do crediting an author and having permission to reuse a work answer different questions?
> Attribution addresses truthful authorship/credit; permission depends on the rights/licence or applicable rules. One does not automatically satisfy the other.

<!-- qard-id: ap-01bcbb25f25cc9d1f64e -->
> [!qard]- What ethical question remains after a proposed use is argued to be legally permitted?
> Whether its effects respect people, creators, fairness, privacy and public welfare under the professional principles; a legal argument is not the whole ethical assessment.

### Don't Lawyerise A Code Of Ethics

<!-- qard-id: ap-aee3785e6eb8e40ee060 -->
> [!qard]- What does “fraus legis” illustrate in the lecture's principle-based reading of ethics?
> Exploiting the literal wording while defeating the rule's intended purpose. The lecture uses it to argue that professional conduct should honour principles and intent, rather than search for an unlisted loophole.
