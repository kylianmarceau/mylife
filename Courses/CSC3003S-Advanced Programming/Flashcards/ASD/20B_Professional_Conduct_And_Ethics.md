## Core Content

### Professions

<!-- qard-id: ap-f8963172c10457feb200 -->
> [!qard]- How do regulated professions (e.g. chartered accountants) differ from software developers?
> They require long education, apprenticeship, licensing, compulsory professional bodies, continuing education and enforced ethics; developers require none of these.

<!-- qard-id: ap-9ec8a44097f352bfd1a2 -->
> [!qard]- What is the status of software developers' codes of ethics?
> Voluntary — not legally enforceable.

<!-- qard-id: ap-c7c1214d86f4ca381ff9 -->
> [!qard]- Why does the ACM oppose licensing developers, and what is the counter-argument?
> It says licensing is premature and wouldn't improve quality. Counter: certified minimum training would improve quality and make professionals accountable.

### Codes Of Ethics

<!-- qard-id: ap-efdfc1215d04eb264b50 -->
> [!qard]- Name five ACM general ethical principles.
> Contribute to human well-being, avoid harm, be honest and trustworthy, be fair and non-discriminatory, respect creative work, privacy and confidentiality.

<!-- qard-id: ap-5e90e28cc416dc4e2ff5 -->
> [!qard]- Name four ACM professional responsibilities.
> High-quality work, maintain competence, respect rules, accept review, assess risks, work only within competence, build secure systems.

<!-- qard-id: ap-1f9990c8843da503e235 -->
> [!qard]- What is the central concern of the ACM leadership principles?
> The public good, plus care for systems integrated into society's infrastructure.

<!-- qard-id: ap-be76451579ef318a0aeb -->
> [!qard]- Name five IEEE code commitments.
> Public safety first, disclose conflicts of interest, reject bribery, accept honest criticism and credit others, work within competence, no discrimination or harassment.

<!-- qard-id: ap-e30b6c3cd40619212fee -->
> [!qard]- Why shouldn't a code of ethics be "lawyerised"?
> Rule-based systems invite gaming ("not forbidden means allowed"); ethics should follow principles and intent.

<!-- qard-id: ap-5001268f3ee41e30d220 -->
> [!qard]- What is *fraus legis*?
> Fraudulently applying the letter of the law against its intent — itself unlawful in principle-based systems.

### Cases

<!-- qard-id: ap-13698b00e3ed5b16d563 -->
> [!qard]- Which ethical principles does the Boeing MCAS case test?
> Avoiding harm, competence, quality, honesty, lawful (FAA) compliance, and care when modifying systems.

<!-- qard-id: ap-7ce9b2dbf1d06f65cb25 -->
> [!qard]- Why was Meta's LLM training on LibGen unethical?
> It used pirated books, violating ACM/IEEE principles on respecting creative work, despite claims of legality.

### IP And Licences

<!-- qard-id: ap-23b958ec40a9013e30fe -->
> [!qard]- What is copyleft?
> Using copyright (e.g. the GPL) to guarantee the freedom to use, modify and distribute software.

<!-- qard-id: ap-2a3af4680af6223dfe1f -->
> [!qard]- What is Richard Stallman's stance on IP?
> Copyright, patents and DRM lock information away; he advocates boycott, not piracy.

<!-- qard-id: ap-4d901a23ee2da88f7256 -->
> [!qard]- What is the main argument for Sci-Hub?
> Free access to science is a right (UDHR Article 27) — civil disobedience against unjust IP law.

<!-- qard-id: ap-94f15771036a26dd6fb7 -->
> [!qard]- Why is "legal" not the same as "ethical"?
> Law balances interests and can be captured by special interests (e.g. apartheid laws were legal).

<!-- qard-id: ap-481f897e81591a4367ff -->
> [!qard]- Name four open-source licences.
> GPL, LGPL, Apache, MIT.

<!-- qard-id: ap-5888cafb19e2f5475613 -->
> [!qard]- Why is plagiarism unethical for developers?
> It violates IP, hides code provenance and limitations, and is dishonest — high probability and high cost, so avoid it.

<!-- qard-id: ap-a898dfd60de2cf607226 -->
> [!qard]- What is intellectual property?
> Legal rights over creations of the mind — copyright, patents and licensing terms (not respected by every country).
